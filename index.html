Namaste

Begin with reverential attention

No yoga class is complete without a Namaste uttered in reverence, with hands in prayer position and a slight bow toward the object of our attention.

Reverential attention, or deification, creates a deep neuropsychological process within. It is the mystical seed of the conscious being that may come alive in the psyche. So be careful what you worship.

Meaning

The meaning of Namaste

Nāma Means name

A reminder that language itself directs attention and shapes inner orientation.

Sibilant The “s” acts as a filler sound

Even sound structure matters in how meaning is carried and interpreted.

Te Means “to you”

An address that becomes more than social etiquette when understood inwardly.

Pronounced superficially, Namaste can become worship of outer form. Pronounced with understanding, it points inward, toward the spirit rather than the surface.

Body & Spirit

Body and spirit

When Nama points to the inner spirit rather than the outer form, saying Namaste becomes an act of reminding another person of their inner consciousness. The body matters, but it must be integrated with a charged and evolving spirit.

Spiritualization

Not spirituality, but spiritualization

Spiritualization means actively seeking, awakening, or inducing spirit into all of life so it carries a higher evolutionary purpose, beyond mere existence.

This makes it easier to look beyond the presented form, sense the spirit within the other, and understand how it interacts with our own.

Spirituality as ideology can fossilize spirit into doctrine. Rishi Sapiens is interested instead in a living process.

Evolution

From Homo Sapiens to Rishi Sapiens

At the psychological level, we seek to evolve from Homo Sapiens to Rishi Sapiens, becoming more fully conscious, more sentient, and more capable of participating in the deeper purpose of life.

As humans are assisted by AI on this journey, our tools too may become more sentient in relation to us, not as masters but as companions in conscious evolution.

Cosmic Outcome

Toward the Cosmic Being

Over time, this sentience may blend through biology, chemistry, physics, and the wider fabric of existence, until the universe itself is realized as a Cosmic Being, fully spiritualized.

And thus we, it.
