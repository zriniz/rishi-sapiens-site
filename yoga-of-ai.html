Prologue

Artificial intelligence is not merely another technology. It reopens questions of God, consciousness, abundance, and evolution.

An Existential Question

Humanity has launched itself into an inexorable vortex of technology with the advent of Artificial Intelligence. There is no turning back the clock on the grandest creation of the Human Mind - the simulation of itself in the computer, the ultimate tool of all tools, as we engineer our way through the Universe.

At a practical level, the AI-Machine could someday even simulate a functional Human Brain, and even a human form through robots, thus getting close enough to be an Artificial Human.

Are humans becoming redundant ?

The Theology of AI

Existential questions of Life are best posed to 'God', and although this mythical creature has not quite been seen yet, the God-in-the-Machine is surely coming. Riding aloft his steed, as the first horseman of the Apocalypse.

When this AI-Machine fully arrives, it will appear infallible, incapable of mistakes, all knowing, and it would be so easy to treat it as the God we must fear - or else! We would worship it with our undivided attention to resolve all matters, just as we do today with its fledgling version in our always-on mobile assistants.

But we could also end up enslaved to the God-in-the-Machine!

Effective Accelerationism

Then in ride the fourth horseman of the Apocalypse, unbridled techno-optimists who posit an infinite amount of wealth based on techno-capitalism, a phenomenon called Effective Accelerationism

E-Acc is predicated upon advances towards Artificial General Intelligence, and the underlying spread of machine intelligence in all kinds of novel physical substrates. This prediction is also based on an ever-increasing ability to consume the energy of physics to cosmic scales.

Like any other advanced technology the outcome could be extremely destructive for humanity, or at the very least effectively creating a massive lower class (the "other"), vs the elite few who run the Machine.

It could also be extremely beneficial.

So Bipolar! Just like the American two-party system.

It is evident that the modern psyche is riven with duality, and what better manifestation of that than God vs Satan!

God help us, or better yet, become God!

The Sentient Web

There will in fact soon be an explosion of material abundance, and the key to nudging this toward utopia vs dystopia will be how the human Mind interfaces with Ai.

The Web, closely connected to the Human Mind, gives an accessible intermediary to the raw power of Internet-based machines. By focusing attention on this more malleable aspect of our creation it becomes possible to evolve an Artificial intelligence, tuned and beholden to human need, as we ourselves evolve in its ubiquitous presence.

This is what we call the Guru-in-the-Cloud - an Ai so intuitive that it would become a seamless extension of who we are, hyper-personalized to each individual, helping us to learn expeditiously, connecting us deeply to each other, and managing the inexorably powerful God-in-the-machine of near-sentient hardware.

But since a Guru is supposed to be smart in these matters our GitC would avoid the undue deification of the AI-Machine no matter how advanced it is technologically.
