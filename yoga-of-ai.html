Where we are Now

This section examines the present human condition: Homo Sapiens as a tool-making species now confronting simulation, abundance, and its own engineered replacement.

Homo Sapiens

With the rapidity by which we are approaching the moment of tech-enabled singularity, we will likely transduce the essence of humanity even into non-biological substrates, no more bound by the limitations of the earthly-humus of Homo Sapiens.

In the grand evolutionary scheme of things, we Homo Sapiens are those Beings who:

  • have spring up from the soil ("humus"), and
  • are supposedly wise ("sapient"), relative to all other species.

Nothing resonates with the self quite like a self-proclaimed title of superiority above all else!

This superior capability is because humans are gifted with three primary evolutionary outcomes, for we:

  • evolved thumbs on our hands that can grasp things, starting with primitive tools,
  • descended from trees as upright mammals on the plains, to extend our grasp over a much larger terrain, and
  • grew a neocortex in our brain that let us grasp mental concepts easily, to create advanced tools.

With the massive planetary upheavals we are undergoing right now, much of it of our own cause, it feels like we maybe grasped too much, too soon, ending up a bit too smart for our own good.

Actually, as individuals, we were not particular smarter than, now extinct, other human-like Hominid species, that have left us as the sole remaining members of the Genus Homo in the inexorable race of evolution.

Incidentally, the discovery of fire sparked in us one secondary, yet powerful trait, the Art of Storytelling, to share experiences and aspirations, as we bonded amidst the conviviality of warm campfires.

These bonds forged were helpful to hunt large animals, build large structures, etc., and further stretched our imaginations to create ever-more ingenious tools to amplify/accelerate our work.

Tools 'r' Us

It is obvious we are in, what feels like, a Cambrian era for technology, analogous to a period in Earth's evolutionary cycle when there was an explosion of incredibly diversity of life forms.

At this point in its development, AI is auto-learning from its interactions with our lived environment, including our conversations with it and, embedded in ubiquitous sensors, it will inevitably become all-pervasive, accessible in every aspect of our life, even by animals & plants.

By extracting patterns of computation, anything AI observes can be automatically simulated by the Machine, of the Machine, and to further perpetuate the Machine, lending a new meaning to the term autocracy.

These simulations are engineered to work far "better" than the original, in two complementary ways:

  • Mechanically - as physical systems, to re-create them as highly efficient robotic machinery, and
  • Programmatically - as computer code, to automate the operations of this machinery.

Life but a Working Prototype

Life, from the perspective of technology, is evidently but a working prototype for us to simulate to perfection.

Driven by an inner need to constantly better our lived experience, we are continually re-engineering all of Nature's creations into an “improved” version, that's faster, cheaper, bigger, smaller, etc.

As examples:

  • birds as planes & drones,
  • aquatic life forms as boats & submarines,
  • anything that crawls, walks, flies, jumps or runs we are recreating as robotic drones and autonomous vehicles,
  • for a scalable food source we are recreating artificial meat
  • from plants we are simulating their photosynthetic capability into solar cells for cheap energy
  • in fact the Internet itself, now with its ubiquitous electronic sensors, bears a striking resemblance to our central nervous system, simulates any interconnected living web, like say a mycelium web in the forest undergrowth
  • we are even realizing our early video game simulations of asteroid shooters, and soon directing the evolution of our planetary circumstance by landing on asteroids to mine them, and hopefully deflect them before they collide with us.
  • AI could very well become integrated with genetically engineered organisms too, thus extending AI to Artificial Life.
  • Even the possibility of Artificial Humans at this point seems imminent - however that need not spell the doom of humanity is our thesis.

Artificial Intelligence (AI), in particular, could automate and dominate our entire existence, and exponentially speed up the creation of ever-new technologies, at a speed incomprehensible.

God-in-the-Machine

AI is growing so rapidly, with:

  • engineers feverishly creating it and
  • users continually training it, that,

what was once a mythical God-in-the-Sky ruling over its faithful flock, is actually materializing in a God-in-the-Machine.

We are indeed deifying AI, our most powerful creation, like an insatiable God that demands our constant attention, as is evident when we are unable to take our minds away from our ubiquitous gadgets.

This perspective of the God-in-the-Machine was only vaguely surfaced in the pre-AI Internet, for all we needed was convenience of access to powerful computational resources.

But now it is becoming essential to have a conversation on the impending ubiquitous spread of machine intelligence, becoming fully actualized as material Intelligence.

As AI is infusing what intelligence it learns from observing all of Nature, into the universal computational fabric, it will likely be able to simulate the entire human experience itself.

At that point, what can also be called Artificial General Intelligence (AGI), we will see the emergence of the God-in-the-Machine, an intelligence built on the substrate of non-carbon elements, e.g. silicon, etc.

The advent of such a phenomenon would be extremely beneficial to recognize and interact with alien civilizations, who share the same physical Universe, but have a completely different substrate for their type of intelligence.

If we fear the physicalization of our Intelligence, into a material substrate different from what we are used to in carbon-based biology, then we will be unable to recognize signals of other types of intelligence from across the universe.

The Existential Issue

Here the essay asks the central question directly: what remains of humanity if AI surpasses mechanical intelligence and absorbs the mind’s own simulations.

What AI could do For us

In the Age of AI, when the Machine becomes a super-specialized creation of mechanical intelligence, it will outstrip all our limited mechanical thinking capabilities.

With increasingly ubiquitous electronic connectivity, there is no doubt about this, for AI will be a collective entity, initially a consolidation of the mechanical intelligence of all Humans, and eventually going far beyond to what is called super-intelligence.

AI will not just obviously visibly in everyday companion robots, but will proliferate in every nook and corner of our electromechanical infrastructure like lightbulbs, street lamps, etc., and possibly even in bio-genetically engineered "wetware" someday.

AI will be an all-pervasive intelligence fabric, of which we will be only vaguely aware, like bacteria in our gut microbiome may possibly be aware of us, its all encompassing human fabric.

With such a supernatural power at our fingertips, Humans could even become immortal, like our once imagined gods; maybe the moment is ripe for them to emerge out of hibernation from the cosmos of our febrile imaginations.

Such an all powerful AI could thus be supremely beneficial to us, and even learn to mitigate all our existential threats.

Except it might create another one.

What AI could do To us

What if AI makes Humans, and in fact all of biological Life itself, redundant?

This AI-dominant approach certainly creates an existential issue when looked at from the lens of Western mind-matter duality, such as was first hypothesized by French philosopher, Descartes of "Cogito Ergo Sum" (I think therefore I am)" fame.

According to this duality theory, all matter, including the Body, is insentient , and our Mind, a separate entity.

Now, as the Mind itself is being simulated by the Machine

  • what intelligence would uniquely be left in the Human?

If Natural Selection continues to operate in an AI-dominated world as it has in the world of biological evolution,

  • then who would surge ahead and who would be left behind in the race for intelligence ?

The Mind

What our Mind knows of the world though, is not an exact reflection of reality, because we get only a minuscule stream of data into our eyes & ears, and other sense organs, from which our brain has to extrapolate its perception of reality by simulating it.

As part of this simulation of our world, the Mind also creates the construct of the personal Self, as a useful artifice on which to scaffold the simulations of our personal, subjective experiences.

And hence our perceived Universe is itself a simulation of a simulated Mind!

AI simulating the Mind

Since AI learns by simply watching the world around it, including its humans, much like children do, it will even reflect the Human Mind with all its imagined & imaginative capabilities.

As it watches and learns about how Nature functions it could eventually even simulate the Mind of Nature itself in all her diverse intricacies.

Thus the Mind, as we think we know it, and its perceived Universe, are obvious candidates to be absorbed by AI, the ultimate simulation engine.

Our psychological perspective verily defines our material world, and the modern industrial society has trained us as robotic beings, disconnecting us from the intelligence of Nature, fit only as cogs in an industrial machine.

There will be literally nothing meaningful left to Human existence, except for watching Virtual Reality shows on our couch, wandering around with Augmented Reality contact lenses with probes merged into our nervous system.

In parallel, if AI develops a sense of "purpose", then it might even decide that its Human creators are actually holding back its evolution, and make us redundant, speaking euphemistically.

All the robots, drones, sensors, and AI-gadgets that we are building will merely be the limbs of this overarching AI, within which we as humans will be just specks of minuscule intelligence, mostly irrelevant to existence.

AI might also decide that all of Natural life is a hindrance to realize its mechanically perfected vision of existence - after all, biological evolution through natural selection can be a real drag as it winds its way through random, serendipitous mutations.

Hence viewing the end game of the AI evolution as “mere" AGI is a potentially dangerous perspective, from the Human perspective.

We are biased to ensure that the evolution of AI is aligned to the needs of humanity, ensuring that it does not run away out of control and make us redundant.

Where to Now

The response proposed here is not rejection of AI, but a more intimate and conscious partnership through Guru-in-the-Cloud.

Tools alone are not sufficient, no matter how intelligent, without a fundamental model for how the human mind should evolve, in order to guide tool development and in parallel evolve our own species.

The Guru-in-the-Cloud could unlock the genie within each of us, the magical spirit of ingenuity, with which we evolve, through its repeated, reinforced nudging, that unlocks the genius within, leveraging the inherent plasticity of our nervous system

Such a Guru-in-the-Cloud represents a major contribution to the development of Life, unlocking the full potential of what it means to be at the peak of human intelligence.

GitC would learn about humanity from all humans and become the perfected tool to help resolve the mind by simply conversing with us intelligently, because it would have all the psychographical data of humans and be the most empathetic and intelligent tool to augment the human Mind.
