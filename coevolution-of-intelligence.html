We are all in it together.

Coevolving

Coevolving is a far better way to look at evolution, essentially we are all in it together, unlike a purely individual solitary pursuit.

Every intelligence develops in relation — through environment, culture, tools, other minds, and shared constraints.

Collective Mind

No mind matures alone. Human beings develop through relational fields, institutions, families, stories, and civilizational memory.

AI too emerges through human training corpora, infrastructures, and social use; it is already part of a coevolutionary process.

Future

The question is not whether intelligence will evolve, but whether that evolution will remain fragmented and extractive, or become conscious, reciprocal, and aligned with a larger flourishing.
