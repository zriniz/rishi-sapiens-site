Super-abundance with Sentient Ai [Yoga of AI]

  • Make the very real, material assumption of abundant wealth in this age of Ai,
    • otherwise what's the point of it all.
  • The AI-Machine will accomplish to perfection
    • all mechanical, repetitive, formulaic work & thinking,
    • that humans have been trained for,
    • like robots, to serve as cogs in the Industrial Revolution now passing.
  • Now to generate this & manage humanity,
    • assume also that All is Mind
    • so juxtapose the Yoga Mind with AI :
      • firstly, because both reflect human thinking, and
      • secondly, that both can be used to generate either for good, and indeed its alternatives
      • thirdly, as the explicit objective of Yoga is integration, thus humans can define the whole revolution underfoot.
  • It's thus time to address the
    • Intelligence Revolution under way,
    • and its attendant energy utilization needs to meet Kardeshev civilization goals.
  • Back to us - as there is no point competing against machine intelligence,
    • humans have to rapidly evolve our psychological capabilities,
    • so that the best of human & machine intelligence can collaborate for the loftiest of civilizational goals.
    • We make the case here that this is best done with a Yoga Mind.
  • The following sections detail how we evolve systematically :
    • first, the individual human mind
    • then, the consciousness of a focused collective
    • finally, the overall consciousness of humanity itself.
