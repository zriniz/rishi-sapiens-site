Karma & Rebirth

Consequence, continuity, and renewal

Karma and rebirth can be approached here as part of a living story-game. Karma shapes the trajectory of experience. Rebirth extends that arc through continuity, recurrence, and renewed opportunity.

The individual life is not treated as a sealed event. It is part of a longer movement of becoming. That movement can be understood psychologically, spiritually, and narratively.

Karma as feedback

Karma is not merely reward and punishment. It is consequence, pattern, and the shaping force of action over time.

Rebirth as second chance

Rebirth can be understood not only across lifetimes but also within a single life as renewal, reinvention, and the possibility of beginning again with greater consciousness.

Why this matters

These ideas support a vision of life that is iterative rather than static. Growth is dramatic, layered, and alive. The human being keeps becoming.
